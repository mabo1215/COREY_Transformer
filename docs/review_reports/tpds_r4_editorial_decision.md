## 2. Decision: **Major Revision**

**Under the decision standards.** All five reviewers independently recommend Major Revision; the decision-matrix row Major/Major/Major/Major maps to Major Revision. The panel mean sits in the Major band, no core dimension is at the floor, and every reviewer states the issues are serious but fixable within one round (EIC: "the path to acceptance is bounded (mostly text and offline analysis; at most about 10 GPU-hours of new measurement)"; R2: "roughly 10-14 GPU-hours ... plus offline analysis"; R3: "roughly 10-20 single-GPU hours of measurement plus reframing"; DA: "None of these requires new science"; R1: "Each required measurement is cheap (minutes to about an hour of GPU time)"). Reject is excluded: nobody recommends it, and all five reports rate the measurement discipline above the TPDS norm. Minor Revision is excluded: the methodology reviewer finds that the headline numbers must be re-derived ("this is not a text-only revision"), three reviewers require the title, abstract, contributions and conclusion to be rescoped, and the result must be re-reviewed.

**Decision rationale.** The panel is unanimous that the manuscript's measurement discipline (recurrence-preserving timing with output checks, BH-corrected F tests, TOST equivalence, placement and L2 interventions, prompt-grouped folds, two timing conventions, three GPUs) exceeds what TPDS usually receives, and that Sec. IX is an exemplary Limitations section. The panel is equally unanimous that the paper's headline claims outrun that evidence in three ways. First, the chunk effect that motivates the table, the cost model and the bound is, by the manuscript's own Sec. IX ("Only the Mamba-1 scan has an optimal chunk that moves with the shape by enough to matter ... measured on a Triton kernel written for this study"), present only on the study's own kernel; the Mamba-2 SSD and Gated DeltaNet kernels have a fixed optimum and the reference CUDA scan's stock rule is within 6.6% of the oracle, yet the title, abstract, contributions and conclusion speak of chunked linear-recurrence kernels in general (R2 F1 and DA C1, both CRITICAL; EIC F2; R3 F7). Second, the headline regrets, tie shares and headroom on the RTX PRO 6000 are reported in the launch-inclusive convention in which 64% of the captured calls are launch-bound, against a per-call oracle taken from a single timing block despite a documented 2% median (90th percentile about 30%) between-block noise (R1 1 and R1 2, both CRITICAL; corroborated by DA C6.4-C6.5); the device-time replication exists for the RTX 3090 and H800 but not for the headline GPU. Third, the parallel/distributed dimension on which TPDS fit rests is argued through shapes rather than measured, and the abstract's "a table tuned for one GPU loses up to 63%" is an unsharded-to-sharded width transfer on the study's own kernel, not a cross-device loss (EIC F3, R2 F8, R3 F2, DA C11). Around these sit consensus concerns that are each fixable: the kernel-level timing regime (warm L2, eager launch floor) is not the serving regime; the cost-model form was selected on the test calls and the baselines are weak; the negative result targets a self-built policy and "cannot help" overstates what the cost side proves; the practical gain is never stated in deployment units; positioning against compile-informed and cross-device performance predictors is missing. Most fixes are text or offline analysis; the two required measurements cost about one GPU-hour; all optional measurements together total roughly 20-31 GPU-hours (24-33 if the engine replay M4c replaces the 2-GPU run M4b).

## 5. Disagreements and arbitration

**D1. Severity and remedy of the scope-evidence mismatch (K1).** R2 and DA rate it CRITICAL and hold that the general claim cannot stand without one independently written, publicly used chunked kernel with a wide chunk range on which the optimum moves (R2: TFLA mLSTM kernels, 4-6 GPU-h; DA: FLA chunk kernels, 4-6 GPU-h). The EIC rates it MAJOR and asks only for attribution in the abstract and contributions; R3 asks (F7, MINOR) to reword the premise and state the production message. *Arbitration: I side with R2 and DA on substance.* The manuscript's own Sec. IX concedes the point in full ("The per-call, predictor and cost-model results therefore rest mainly on Mamba-1, measured on a Triton kernel written for this study") while the title, abstract, Contribution 1 and Sec. X do not; the EIC's attribution fix is necessary but does not make the general title true. Resolution carried into the review: rescoping of title/abstract/contributions/conclusion is mandatory (M1a); the general framing may be retained only if the public-kernel measurement (M1b) shows a moving optimum; otherwise the thesis becomes "shape-keyed tuning is enough and often unnecessary", which R2 explicitly calls publishable.

**D2. Whether a measured multi-GPU deployment is required for TPDS fit (K3).** The EIC prefers one 2-GPU tensor-parallel confirmation (4-8 GPU-h) but accepts reframing as the alternative; R3 "strongly recommends" a real TP = 2 run and proposes a full engine replay (8-10 GPU-h, "a null result ... is still the right thing to publish"); R2 holds that the TP table-transfer comparison is a strawman whatever is measured, because TP degree is fixed at deployment and retuning at the shard width costs seconds; DA asks to narrow the keywords and abstract. *Arbitration: I side with the EIC's either/or, and carry R2's point as mandatory in both branches.* Venue fit is the editor's call and the editor accepts the reframing; R2's objection is independent of measurement and the manuscript's Sec. VII-F figures (1.2 s to profile six chunks at twelve keys) support it. Resolution: the abstract wording changes are required (M4a); the author chooses between the 2-GPU confirmation (M4b), R3's engine replay (M4c, which subsumes M4b and also serves K6) and the text-only reframing; in every branch the TP result is presented as cold-start value with the cost of retuning stated next to it.

**D3. The cost side of the no-gain bound (K5).** The EIC lists the headroom-versus-decision-cost framing as a strength ("turns 'should dispatch read the data?' into two measurable quantities"); DA C3 calls part (a) definitional and the synchronization verdict a comparison "a competent implementation would never pay"; R3 F6 says no serving engineer would ship a blocking synchronization. *Arbitration: I side with R3 and DA on wording, and the EIC's framing survives as the secondary statement.* The manuscript itself says in Sec. IV that for fused, overlapped or device-side mechanisms "the argument rests on information rather than cost"; the abstract's categorical "cannot help" is therefore stronger than the paper's own logic. Resolution: replace "cannot help" with the quantitative bound stated for zero decision cost, reorder Sec. IV and the abstract so the information result leads (M7c); the zero-sync measurement (M7e) is optional.

**D4. Adequacy of the entropy-tree test (K8).** R1 6 holds the test is weak by construction and asks for bootstrap CIs, an exact-key stratified permutation test and the conditional MI given the exact key; DA C8 agrees the tree has too little capacity but holds the paper already answers adequately through the exact-shape headroom, so the tree is "redundant evidence presented as the decisive comparison". *Arbitration: both, no conflict in substance.* R1's additions are offline and cheap and come from the methodology expert; DA's reordering is adopted. Resolution: M7d.

**D5. Severity of the eager-mode Mamba-2 evidence (K2).** The EIC rates it MINOR with an optional 1-2 GPU-h CUDA-graph re-timing or a text caveat; R2 F4 rates it MAJOR and asks for CUDA-graph capture of SSD and GDN on all 2360 / 1770 captured calls (3 GPU-h); R3 F4 rates it MAJOR; DA Sec. 5(3) notes the fixed-optimum conclusion "survives" but the stated mechanism "is a description of the harness". *Arbitration: I side with R2 (domain expertise) that CUDA-graph replay is the correct protocol and that all Mamba-2 conclusions are presently eager-mode conclusions, and with the EIC that the fixed optimum will very likely survive.* Resolution: M3b is strongly recommended but not required; the launch-bound default fix (M3c, offline) and the eager-mode scope statement (M3d) are required in every case because they correct a concrete implementer-facing rule.

**D6. Whether the cost model has practical value (K6).** DA C9 argues the model fails the paper's own break-even against a six-chunk profile (pay-back after about 2,000-7,000 calls per key, i.e. minutes) and that recommending a 7-15 us-per-call policy while dismissing a 0.45-2.2 us one is an internal tension; R3 F3 holds the niche (keys that cannot be enumerated before serving, targets one cannot time, pruning to top-2) is real but never argued; the EIC and R3 list the cold-key default and the compile-facts-only H800 transfer (3.8%) among the strengths. *Arbitration: the disagreement is about framing, not facts; I side with R3's framing and require DA's arithmetic to be answered in the text.* The VII-H sentence DA relies on is in the manuscript verbatim. Resolution: compute the model's break-even against per-key profiling and the startup cost of profiling the bucket grid (M6b), state the niche precisely and name the top-2 hybrid as the shipped recommendation (M6c), and address the headroom-versus-model-regret tension explicitly.

**D7. Significance weighting.** Scores range from 48 (DA) to 66 (R1) for the same evidence. This is a perspective difference (R1 values the measurement guidance; DA and R3 value what a serving system would change today), not a factual dispute, and it does not alter the decision: even the DA's weighted total (60.0) lies in the Major Revision band.

## 7. Acceptance criteria for the revised manuscript

The revised manuscript will be sent back to the panel. It will be acceptable if it demonstrates all of the following:

- **A1 (scope).** The title, abstract, contribution list, first paragraph of Sec. VII and Sec. X attribute the 8.5x spread, the 13-24% batched static regret, the 63% sharding loss and the 0.9/3.2/3.5% regrets to the Triton Mamba-1 scan of this study (1.23x the reference scan), and state next to them that Mamba-2 SSD (chunk 256, at most 0.24% loss), Gated DeltaNet (chunk 64, library limit) and the reference CUDA scan (stock rule within 6.6%) need no dispatch; "defaults suffice" is presented as a finding. Either the general framing is supported by a public kernel with a moving optimum (M1b) or it is narrowed.
- **A2 (convention and oracle).** Headline regrets, tie shares and headroom for the RTX PRO 6000 are reported in device time (or in both conventions, with the convention named in the abstract), against a per-call oracle defined on at least three shuffled rounds; Tables II, III and the headroom are re-derived accordingly.
- **A3 (claims supported by tables).** Every headroom and regret number in the abstract has a tabulated entry: the superseded rows of Table S-headroom-noise are replaced by re-timed rows with per-shape held-out maxima and above-synchronization counts in both conventions; the three regret estimators are defined in Sec. VI-D and each table names the one it uses; the abstract carries mean and p95 for all three GPUs.
- **A4 (model form and baselines).** The form-selection step is clean (form-blind numbers lead, or the form is re-selected on calibration and leave-one-key-out data) and a baseline table on all three GPUs includes the three-parameter restriction, nearest key, log-linear interpolation, a length-only tree, a learned regressor on the same calibration timings and the TFLA expression.
- **A5 (parallel dimension).** The abstract separates cross-device loss (14-49%) from unsharded-to-sharded loss (63%) and names the kernel; "mixed GPU fleets", "LLM serving" and "tensor-parallel" claims match what was measured; either one measured TP deployment is reported or the claims are reframed as single-device shapes produced by parallel deployments, with the simulated nature of the trace study stated.
- **A6 (timing regime).** The warm-L2 and eager-mode caveats are either measured (cold-L2 re-timing; CUDA-graph capture) or stated at abstract/claim level; the launch-bound default of the work-term model is corrected; the Triton `do_bench` L2-flush discrepancy is stated.
- **A7 (negative result).** The Introduction states whether any published runtime proposes value-dependent dispatch for dense chunked kernels; "cannot help" is replaced by the quantitative bound at zero decision cost; the information result leads; the entropy test carries CIs and an exact-key stratified test; Sec. III is at most half a column.
- **A8 (practical value).** A deployment-units table (ms per request and share of prefill and total GPU time per kernel, length and batch), the model-versus-profiling break-even, a deployment recipe box and p95/p99 of the top-2 hybrid are present.
- **A9 (positioning).** The related-work paragraphs cite and position against the compile-informed, cross-device, shape-generic and learned predictors and Triton's perf-model pruning; the novelty sentence in Sec. V is scoped accordingly.
- **A10 (venue and hygiene).** All MUST FIX items (V1-V6) are done; the body stays within 14 pages; every number mismatch in N1 is reconciled; bibliography and typesetting items are fixed.
