\section*{Scope and Inputs}

This file opens revision cycle tpds-r4. It synthesizes a fresh, independent five-reviewer panel
(Editor-in-Chief, methodology, domain, serving-practitioner perspective, devil's advocate) that
reviewed the current manuscript (main body, 14 formatted pages; Supplementary Material, 16 pages)
jointly from the sources alone, without reference to earlier rounds, against the standard of the
IEEE Transactions on Parallel and Distributed Systems (regular paper, double-anonymous, 14
formatted pages including references). The reports and the editorial decision letter (with the
arbitration of reviewer disagreements and acceptance criteria A1--A10) are in
\texttt{docs/review\_reports/tpds\_r4\_*.md}. Each concern cites the findings it merges (EIC F$n$,
R1 $n$, R2 F$n$, R3 F$n$, DA C$n$). Nothing from earlier cycles is carried over.

Effort classes: (a) text only; (b) offline analysis of existing result files; (c) new GPU
measurement, with GPU-hour estimate and host. Items of class (c) are run only after the author
selects them. Every (c) item except M2a and M2b has an (a) or (b) fallback; M2a and M2b are
required because they resolve the two CRITICAL methodology findings at about one GPU-hour.
Numbering follows the manuscript: Sec.~I--X, Results VII-A to VII-I, Tables~I (value invariance),
II (oracle regret), III (predictors), IV (reference scan), V (routed study), VI (replication).
